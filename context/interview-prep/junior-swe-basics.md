# Junior SWE interview preparation (starter notes)

Starter notes written when the harness was set up. Edit freely: this is the only source
`kb-answer` uses for general interview questions, so add what you learn.

## Typical stages
1. Short phone or video call with HR/recruiter (motivation, salary expectation, availability)
2. Technical interview (fundamentals, your projects, sometimes a small coding task)
3. Meeting with the team or manager (fit, how you work)

## Technical topics to prepare
- One main language well (e.g. Python, Java, C#, or JavaScript/TypeScript): basics, data types, collections, error handling
- Data structures and algorithms basics: arrays, lists, maps, sorting, big-O at a simple level
- Git: branching, merging, pull requests
- SQL basics: SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY
- HTTP/REST basics and how a web app talks to a database
- Testing basics: what a unit test is and why it matters
- Be ready to explain one or two of your own projects: what it does, what you decided, what went wrong

## Questions to expect
- Why software development, and why this company?
- Tell me about a project you built.
- Describe a bug you fixed and how you found it.
- How do you learn something new?

## Questions to ask them
- What does onboarding look like for a junior?
- Who would I work with day to day, and is there a mentor?
- What does the tech stack and development process look like?

## Practical (Netherlands)
- Know your salary range and notice period/start date.
- Confirm the language of the interview (Dutch or English) ahead of time.
- Check the contact person and the interview location or video link beforehand.
